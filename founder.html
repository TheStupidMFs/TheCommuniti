01 / My story

Design was the entry point. Building complete brands became the work.

I began working independently as a graphic designer in 2015, helping startups, artists, events and established businesses develop stronger visual identities. Duke Bakery became my first international commercial client, where I designed gift cards, menus and promotional banners across customer touchpoints.

My career grew through the Los Angeles music and event industry. With Steejays Entertainment, I created flyers, merchandise and promotional campaigns for more than 15 club events and three festivals. The work taught me how design, marketing, production and live experiences come together to make a brand recognizable.

I later expanded into creative production—coordinating artist sessions, developing music-video concepts, supporting location shoots and helping bring ideas from the first conversation to the finished release.

02 / The Communiti

Built from the perspective of both a designer and an entrepreneur.

As an artist and business owner, I understood how difficult it could be to coordinate branding, media, marketing, websites, merchandise and production without one person understanding the full vision.

I founded The Communiti to bring those disciplines together. The goal is to listen closely, understand what a business is trying to become and strengthen every visual touchpoint around that idea.

More recently, I built the Web3 company The Stupid MFs—developing its NFT collection, visual identity, websites, merchandise, community products and creative direction.
